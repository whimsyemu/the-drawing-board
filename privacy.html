Privacy

My Drawing Board · whimsyemu.com · effective 27 August 2026

The short version: everything you write stays on your device. The app has no account, no sign-up, and no server of ours. We never see, collect, or receive your writing.

What the app stores

Your notes, lists, settings and (if you set one) your password are saved only in the app's own storage on your phone, tablet or computer. Your password is stored as a one-way hash, not as text.

What leaves your device

Nothing is sent to Whimsy Emu. The app makes no network requests of its own.

On Apple devices, the app can keep your board the same across your own devices through your iCloud account (Apple's key-value storage). That data travels between your devices and Apple only, under Apple's privacy terms, and only when you are signed into iCloud.

If you use the "download a backup" or "copy everything" buttons, the file or text goes exactly where you send it — and nowhere else.

What we don't do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, no selling or sharing of data. There is nothing to sell: we don't have it.

Beta testing (TestFlight / Play testing)

If you install a test version through Apple's TestFlight or Google Play testing, Apple or Google may share crash logs and basic usage statistics with us under their own terms. That never includes what you write.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
